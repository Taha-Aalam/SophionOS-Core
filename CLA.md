# SophionOS Contributor License Agreement ("CLA")

Thank you for your interest in contributing to SophionOS. In order to keep the
SophionOS codebase sustainably dual-licensed — the open-source **AGPL-3.0**
community version and the **proprietary Sophion Cloud / SophionOS Business**
commercial offerings — this CLA clarifies the rights you grant to the SophionOS
project owners when you submit a contribution.

**Please read this document carefully before signing.**

---

## Plain-English summary (not a substitute for the full text)

- You keep the copyright on your contribution. You are *licensing* it to us.
- We may use, modify, and distribute your contribution under the AGPL-3.0
  open-source license **and** under our commercial licenses (SophionOS
  Business / Sophion Cloud and any future offerings).
- You confirm you actually own what you submit.
- The CLA covers your submissions to SophionOS — nothing else. It does not
  give you any rights over SophionOS's existing code beyond what the project
  license already grants.

**"You"** (or **"Your"**) means the copyright owner of the Contribution, or a
legal entity authorized to act on the copyright owner's behalf.

**"Contribution"** means any original work of authorship, including any
modification or addition to an existing work, that You intentionally submit
to SophionOS for inclusion in the project. "Submit" includes any form of
electronic, verbal, or written communication (pull requests, issues, mailing
lists, code review, or any other channels managed by the project), excluding
communication conspicuously marked "not a contribution."

**"Project Owners"** means the maintainers and legal owners of the SophionOS
project (listed in `GOVERNANCE.md` / `SUPPORT.md`), and their successors and
assigns.
