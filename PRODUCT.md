# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary users are overwhelmed professionals (25–40) juggling work, health, goals, and knowledge across too many apps. Secondary users track self-improvement and want progress visibility. Tertiary users want practical organization with low setup.

They open the dashboard in daylight or evening at a desk, often mid-context-switch, to answer: what needs attention, where is the system unhealthy, and what is still active.

## Product Purpose

SophionOS Core is a life operating system and system of record for interconnected life domains (PARA-style areas, goals, projects, tasks, notes, resources, contacts). The dashboard is the calm command surface: surface focus and risk first, then active entities, without forcing users to assemble databases.

Success is reduced cognitive load, trustworthy interconnected data, and a tool that disappears into the daily workflow.

## Brand Personality

Wise, calm, precise. Second-mind clarity rather than hustle productivity. Editorial restraint with purposeful structure.

## Anti-references

- Generic neon “AI SaaS” dark dashboards
- Notion template maximalism and blank-canvas paralysis
- Hero-metric KPI walls with decorative gradients
- Loud gamification, confetti, and bounce-heavy motion
- Nested card stacks and identical icon-card grids as decoration

## Design Principles

1. **Wisdom over information** — show relationships and priorities, not raw dumps.
2. **Calm command center** — hierarchy answers “what needs me now?” before catalogs.
3. **Earned familiarity** — standard product affordances; density when useful.
4. **Restraint by default** — one accent family, semantic color for state, not decoration.
5. **Motion is state** — short, purposeful transitions; no page-load theater.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support reduced motion, keyboard paths for primary actions, and color that is not the sole carrier of status. Prefer skeletons and clear error recovery over spinners-only loading.
