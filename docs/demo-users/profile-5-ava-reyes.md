# Profile 5 — Ava Reyes, Social Media Marketing & Sales (Agency Founder)

**Persona**: 31-year-old founder of **"Reyes Social"**, a boutique social media marketing agency with 3 retainer clients (a D2C skincare brand, a coffee chain, and a fitness startup). She runs both sides of the business — the **marketing side** (content calendars, campaigns, influencer programs, analytics) and the **sales side** (outbound prospecting, pitching new clients, growing MRR). Captures content ideas and trending formats constantly via the **browser extension** and **mobile share sheet** while doom-scrolling TikTok/Instagram.

**Why this profile sells**: Showcases features the other 4 profiles don't:
1. **Recurring content-cadence tasks** — daily posts, weekly content planning, weekly analytics review, monthly client reporting. The "zero-maintenance" promise for anyone with a content schedule.
2. **Contacts as a sales CRM** — leads → prospects → clients flowing through a pipeline, with follow-up intervals driving the tracker.
3. **Topics as content pillars** — the exact framework social media marketers use (Education, Entertainment, BTS, UGC, Promo) as the connective tissue.
4. **Capture-to-inbox for trending content** — sounds, formats, competitor posts land in `inbox` and get triaged.
5. **Influencer/collaborator contacts with project roles** — not just clients, but creators she manages.
